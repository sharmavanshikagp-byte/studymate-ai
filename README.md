# 📚 StudyMate AI
### 🌐 Live Demo

👉 **[Try StudyMate AI](https://studymate-ai-ses2rxzrx69miqtjwubtpz.streamlit.app)**

> Upload your study material, generate summaries, ask questions, create exam questions, and test yourself with AI-powered quizzes.

> Your smart little study companion ✨

StudyMate AI is an AI-powered study assistant that helps students turn their PDF study material into simple, exam-ready learning resources.

## ✨ Features

- 📄 **PDF Notes** — Upload your study material as a PDF.
- ✍️ **OCR Support** — Extract text from scanned PDF pages.
- 🧠 **Smart Summary** — Generate structured, exam-ready summaries.
- 💬 **Ask My Notes** — Ask questions based on your uploaded material.
- ❓ **Exam Questions** — Generate university-style questions.
- 🎯 **Quiz Mode** — Create and attempt AI-generated MCQs.
- 📊 **Quiz History** — Keep track of quiz performance during a session.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- Python
- Streamlit
- Ollama
- Gemma AI
- PyPDF
- PyMuPDF
- Tesseract OCR
- Pillow

## 🤖 AI

StudyMate AI uses a locally running Ollama model to generate summaries, answer questions, create exam questions, and generate quizzes.

The application is designed to keep study material processing local rather than relying on a paid external AI API.
